## Recommendation for the consumer owner

The missing real V2 serving-path replacement/refusal proof is durably tracked at [swxtchio/swx-abbe#1556](https://github.com/swxtchio/swx-abbe/issues/1556), filed from this dated inspection. The producer dependency and cohort/migration decision remain under swxtchio/swx-abbe#1227.

Keep the inspected V2 serving-path work; a recommendation to add V2 helper methods alone would miss what is already present. Before rollout, execute the actual gateway/adapter/SessionPool path against the attested release at the architecture selected by ADR0031. Capture the exact gateway-derived top-level prompt ID, admission receipt, positive wait coverage, all terminal assistant/tool projections and the observed native cursor persisted by the durable owner. Exercise same-ID retry, missing pointers/cursor refusal, same-identity native-store replacement preserving prior sentinel/history and native durable ordering, and writer/actor-generation plus idle-turn/sibling-session fences. Keep the completed-history restart proof distinct from unavailable ambiguous in-flight recovery; `wait` remains join-only.

No persistent native-store cohort was enumerated in this task. Source support for retaining V1 bindings is not a census and cannot prove either zero V1 turns or safe migration. Require a positively scoped inventory of every retained store/binding and V1 turn presence before cutover. A V1 cohort needs ADR-owner-approved in-place migration semantics preserving native IDs and order. The accepted ADR amendment retains supplied snapshots as ordinary native prompt input and audit history; customer projection omission and provider-context compaction are separate consumer obligations.
